# Incremental Reading

Right-click a folder and choose **New topic**, or press Alt+D. A topic can hold extracts, and each extract can have its own children.

Select text and press Alt+A to extract it. The source keeps a colored anchor; Ctrl-click the anchor to jump back. When several cards share the same anchor, you pick which one to open.

Select text and press Alt+S to create a test card. The question keeps the source text and the selected part goes into the answer; both stay editable.

The three switches at the bottom left of the workspace toggle extract, test-card, and topic highlights. Every command can be rebound under **Settings → Shortcuts**.
