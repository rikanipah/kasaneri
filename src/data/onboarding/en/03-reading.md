# Working Through To-do

To-do pushes topics and extracts by due date and priority. You can edit the material while reading it. Open it with the book icon in the right toolbar or with Alt+L.

It is **read-only** by default: press Alt+E or the **Edit** button at the bottom left to edit, and press it again to leave editing.

The keys below only work while you are not editing:

- **1 Read**: push the next round further out using the growth factor.
- **2 Later**: push it back sooner, for material you did not finish.
- **3 Move to end**: bring it back at the end of the current queue without changing its state.
- **4 Done**: stop pushing it; the content stays in the library.
- **5 Enqueue for review**: put a test card back into the review queue and start scoring it from the beginning.

Intervals, priority, and the daily amount are set under **Settings → Default To-do**, and per deck under a folder's **Settings**.
