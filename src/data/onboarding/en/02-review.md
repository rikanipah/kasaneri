# Reviewing Test Cards

Every folder in the Directory Tree is a deck; nested folders are subdecks. Select a folder and click **Review** in the right toolbar, or right-click it and choose **Start review**. Only test cards enter review.

Settings are inherited from the nearest parent deck that overrides them, then from **Settings → Default Review**. To change one deck only, right-click its folder and choose **Review settings**.

Press Space to reveal the answer, then rate 1 Again, 2 Hard, 3 Good, 4 Easy. Hard and Easy are hidden by default and can be enabled in Default Review.

Reaching the leech threshold by repeated Again ratings tags the card and moves it to To-do for revision. You can also do this by hand with **More → Pause to To-do**.

When you cannot yet decide whether to keep a card, use **More → On hold** and decide later in the Card Browser.

Four ways to stop a card: **Pause to To-do** leaves review and keeps pushing it in To-do; **On hold** leaves both queues; **Bury** only postpones it past the next day boundary; **Done** stops scheduling it.
